What it tells you

For largemouth, smallmouth and spotted bass, and crappie, on any named lake or reservoir in the USA.

Places named in reports

When a report names a creek, bridge, island or dam that's on the lake map, it gets a numbered pin with the report's own words. Nothing else gets a pin.

Past years, this time of year

No recent report for your lake? It says so, then shows what was reported there in past years within three weeks of your date.

What anglers report

The baits, colors, methods, depths and cover named in recent reports for your lake, with how many reports said each one and who said it.

Easy to read in bright sun

Big, high-contrast type and buttons you can hit with wet hands. After sunset it turns red and amber to save your night vision.

Conditions at the lake

Water temperature from a USGS gauge on the lake when it has one, hour-by-hour wind, sky, air temperature and pressure, plus sunrise, sunset and the moon, up to 15 days out.

Getting there

Go here to any pinned place or boat ramp right on the app's map: your boat, the distance and the direction, like your boat's GPS. Every report opens right in the app.

How it works

  1. Search for your lake

    Type a few letters of any lake or reservoir in the USA.

  2. Pick bass or crappie and a date

    Today or your trip day. Dates up to 15 days out use the weather forecast.

  3. Read what's working

    See the baits, depths and places anglers report, tap a pin for the report's own words, and go there.

Where it comes from

Allen Fish Finder shows only what is reported, measured or forecast, with who said it and when. It adds no spots, baits or ratings of its own.

  • Angler reports: state wildlife agency and guide fishing reports, fishing forums, news stories (through the GDELT Project) and public Bluesky posts
  • Lake outlines, creeks, bridges, dams and ramps: OpenStreetMap
  • Maps: USGS The National Map and OpenStreetMap
  • Weather, wind and pressure: Open-Meteo
  • Water temperature: USGS gauges
  • Sun and moon times: worked out on your phone

A report tells you what worked for someone else on their day. Fish move, so use it as your starting point and adjust to what you find on the water.
