Privacy

No accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your theme choice (Sun, Calm or Night), and where your last lake is, so the right theme shows when the app opens.
  • Lakes, forecasts and map pieces you've opened, so they load fast and still come up with a weak signal.
  • Your location, only if you tap the location button. It's used to show where you are on the map and isn't sent anywhere.

You can remove all of it by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings.

What the app asks other services for

To build a forecast, your phone asks these free public services directly. The requests carry the lake name you search for and the lake's position, not who you are. Like any website, the services also see your internet address.

  • Lake search and outlines: Photon by Komoot, and Nominatim from OpenStreetMap
  • Creeks, bridges and docks: the Overpass API from OpenStreetMap
  • Weather: Open-Meteo
  • Water temperature: USGS Water Services
  • Maps: USGS The National Map and OpenStreetMap
  • Recent angler reports: the GDELT Project's news search and Bluesky's public post search, given only the lake name

Links that leave the app

Directions open in Google Maps. Angler reports open on the state, guide, forum, news or Bluesky page they came from, and the report links open YouTube, Reddit and web searches. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

This website

These pages use no cookies and no analytics, and their fonts come from this site rather than another service. The site and the app are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' internet addresses for security.
