    Frequently asked questions

    How do I request a quote?

    Use the contact form or book a discovery call. We reply within one business day.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    Credit and debit cards, PayPal, and ACH bank transfer. See Billing.

    How do I log in to the client portal?

    Use the Client Login link at the top of any page. Contact support if you need a password reset.

    What's included in a Website Care Plan?

    Core and plugin updates, daily backups, uptime monitoring, security scans and an hour of content edits each month.

    Do you sign NDAs?

    Yes, we're happy to sign a mutual NDA before discussing project details.

    Who owns the code and drawings?

    You do. Ownership transfers on final payment, as written in every proposal.
