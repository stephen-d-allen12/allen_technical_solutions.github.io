    SpeedPlot

    Windows · Linux · macOS
    Out now

    Fast plotting and analysis for engineering CSV logs from ECUs, CAN buses, DAQ systems, dynos or test benches. Drop in a folder of logs and SpeedPlot finds the time column, joins the files into one timeline and lays out your plots.

    • Imports 626 MB of logs (a million rows, 106 channels) in under a second on a 2015 laptop
    • Reads comma, semicolon, tab or pipe files, including units rows and decimal commas
    • Up to 8 linked plots with A/B cursors, statistics between cursors, X-Y plots and math channels
    • Offline license key, so it works on locked-down PCs with no activation server
    • Version 1.0.3
    • Perpetual license with 12 months of updates
    • Single user or whole site
    Pricing coming soon
