Who we are

Built by an engineer, for people who build

Allen Technical Solutions is led by Stephen D. Allen, an engineering and controls leader with 26+ years of experience across automotive, industrial and transportation engineering.

Stephen has led global engineering teams, managed complex technical programs, and built real-time control systems, calibration strategies and automated test environments. He is known for reducing technical risk, improving product quality and turning customer requirements into engineering solutions that scale.

  • B.S. Electrical & Communication Engineering, 3.92 GPA
  • Six Sigma Green Belt projects in release and test processes
  • Cybersecurity subject-matter expert for controls software releases
  • Five years supporting OEMs across Central Europe from Germany

Allen Technical Solutions combines that controls and test foundation with modern AI-assisted tools, which lets a small, engineer-led firm take on software, apps and web projects alongside traditional engineering, with every deliverable reviewed by an engineer.

Based in Indianapolis, Indiana. Connect on LinkedIn.
