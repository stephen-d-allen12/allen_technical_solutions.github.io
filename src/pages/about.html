Who we are

Engineering for people who build

Allen Technical Solutions is an engineering firm with almost 30 years of experience in controls, automation, test and validation across the automotive, industrial and transportation industries.

Our background is in leading engineering teams, running complex technical programs, and building real-time control systems, calibration strategies and automated test environments. We focus on reducing technical risk, improving product quality and turning customer requirements into solutions that scale.

  • Electrical and communication engineering foundation
  • Six Sigma-style process improvement
  • Functional safety and cybersecurity for software releases
  • Customer support across North America and Europe

We pair that foundation with modern design, simulation and automation tools, so we can take on software, apps and web projects alongside traditional engineering, with every deliverable reviewed and tested before it ships.

Based in Indianapolis, Indiana, working with clients anywhere.
